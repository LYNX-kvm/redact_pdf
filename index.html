How it works: each page is scanned for the text you entered, matching regions are blacked out, then every page is flattened into a fresh image-based PDF. This means the original text underneath is fully destroyed — not just visually covered — so it can't be recovered by copying text or lifting the black box. The tradeoff is the output PDF is no longer text-searchable/selectable.
